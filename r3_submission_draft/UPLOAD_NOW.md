# Moodle upload package — R3

1. Open https://moodle.dilab.ca/mod/assign/view.php?id=3809.
2. Upload `release_submission.md` and `evaluation_adapter.json` in the files area.
3. Paste the contents of `moodle_online_text_draft.md` into **Online text**.
4. Click **Save changes** to retain a draft. Do not use final submission until
   the Supabase database has finished restoring and the live checks are rerun.
